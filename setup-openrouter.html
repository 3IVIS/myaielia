Set up OpenRouter

One key gives Aielia access to many AI models. Seven steps, about five minutes, with a spending limit so it can never cost more than you decide.

  1. Create your account

    Go to openrouter.ai and choose Sign in. Sign up with Google, GitHub or email.

  2. Add a little credit

    Open openrouter.ai/settings/credits, click Add credits and buy $5 to $10.

    Credit is prepaid, so Aielia can never spend more than you have added. Leave Auto Top-Up off, which is its default.

  3. Create a key

    Open openrouter.ai/workspaces/default/keys, or choose Keys in the dashboard menu, and click + New key. Name it Aielia and choose an Expiration.

  4. Set the key’s spending limit

    Under Key limit, open the Credit limit list and choose Custom amount, then type 5. Set Reset limit every… to a month, then click Create.

    The list also offers $50, $250 and $1,000. Do not choose No limit. When the limit is reached, requests with this key stop until it resets or you raise it.

  5. Copy the key

    The key starts with sk-or-v1-. Copy it now and keep it private. Anyone who has it can spend your credit.

  6. Optional: add a guardrail

    Choose Guardrails in the menu and click + New guardrail. Pick Budget and continue. Name it, fill in Credit Limit (USD) and Reset limit every…, continue, then assign it to your key.

    This is an extra cap on top of step 4, so you can skip it.

  7. Paste the key into Aielia

    In Aielia open Settings, set the provider to OpenRouter, paste the key into API key and save. Send a message to check it works.

    Aielia keeps the key in your browser or on your computer and has no account of its own.

Checked in the OpenRouter dashboard in November 2026. Button names can change; the idea stays the same: prepaid credit plus a limit on the key. Check your balance any time at openrouter.ai/settings/credits.
