**First, get a quick handle on scope**
- How many chapters/units or drug classes are you covering?
- Is it mostly mechanism-based (pharmacodynamics/kinetics) or heavy on memorization (drug names, doses, side effects)?

**A rough approach that works well for pharm specifically:**
1. **Day 1-2**: Skim everything once just to see the map — don't try to learn yet, just know what exists.
2. **Group by drug class, not by lecture order.** Pharmacology rewards pattern recognition — learn the "prototype" drug in each class deeply, then just note how the others differ (exceptions, unique side effects).
3. **Build one big comparison table per system** (e.g., all antihypertensives: class, mechanism, key side effects, contraindications). This is usually the highest-yield study method for pharm.
4. **Spaced repetition for drug names/doses** — flashcards (Anki) started early, reviewed daily, since raw recall decays fast.
5. **Last 3-4 days**: practice questions only, and re-drill whatever you keep missing.
